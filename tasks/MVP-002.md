# Task: MVP-002 — Playable view after joining

## Goal
After joining a server, the player sees their own car from behind and the server's synced world, instead of a fixed camera that points away from the spawn grid.

## Changes
- **ChaseCamera** (Presentation, on the Client scene's Main Camera): follows the local player's networked car (`LocalPlayer.Vehicle`, set by the owning `VehicleNetworkDriver`) or an offline auto-simulated car. It follows yaw only and smooths position frame-rate independently. With no car it leaves the camera where it is (join screen, benchmark).
- **NetworkWorldView** (Presentation, new `NetworkWorld` object): renders `ClientBootstrap.World.Replica` with one mesh per non-empty chunk. It remeshes a chunk and its face neighbours when a snapshot replaces the chunk or a delta bumps its revision, at most 12 chunks per frame. It also attaches `DebrisEffects` so removed blocks show debris. The surface is lifted 2 cm above the arena ground box, since both top out at y = 0.
- `ClientBootstrap.World` exposes the synced world while connected.

## Not done
- Build mode on the phone does not send edits yet (`BuildTargeting` targets the local demo terrain; only bots send `SendEdit`).
- Placed blocks have no colliders on the server or the client; the arena ground box is the driving surface.
