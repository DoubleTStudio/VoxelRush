# Task: UI-001 — In-game menu: settings and leave

## Goal
While connected, players can adjust controls, graphics and volume, and leave the world, without restarting the app.

## Changes
- **`GameMenu`** (Presentation/UI, on `NetworkWorld`): a pause button (top-right, inside the safe area) opens:
  - sliders for steering sensitivity and button size (`ControlSettings` bounds),
  - Graphics: Auto / Low / Medium / High,
  - effects volume,
  - Resume and Leave world.
- **Applying settings:** they apply immediately to `DrivingInputReader`, `TouchControlsScaler`, `QualityController` and `AudioListener`. A fixed tier turns adaptation off; Auto turns it back on with the device's classified tier. Settings are saved through `DeviceSettings` (SAVE-008).
- **`ClientBootstrap.Leave()`** saves the chunk cache, drops the connection and returns to the join screen without a "Disconnected" error. The online world is forgotten, so reconnect doesn't rejoin it. Returning to the join screen now also clears `RaceClient.Current`.
- While the menu is open, camera gestures and build taps are ignored.

## Verification
- PlayMode 39/39: Client scene has `GameMenu`; Apply sets the tier and adaptation and restores Auto; volume. EditMode 587/587.

## Risks / rollback
Client-only. Rollback: revert.
