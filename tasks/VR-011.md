# Task: VR-011 — Arcade Rigidbody vehicle with suspension, drift and boost

## Goal
A raycast-suspension arcade car. DoD (BACKLOG): stable over static track.

## Prerequisites / relevant docs
VR-010 (`DrivingInput`), VEHICLE_NETWORKING (Vehicle), GAMEPLAY_RULES (Vehicles: arcade acceleration, speed-based steering, drifting, boost, reset when stuck), skills/unity-gameplay (pure tuning separated from MonoBehaviour).

## In scope / out of scope
In: suspension, drive/brake/reverse, rolling resistance, speed-scaled steering, lateral grip + handbrake drift, boost energy/cooldown, airborne upright assist, flip/stuck reset, test track + prefab, stability tests.
Out: networking/prediction (VR-012), wiring the touch HUD to a car in `Client.unity` (VR-012 spawns server-owned cars), camera, jump assist, vehicle damage (M4), audio/VFX, final art and tuning on device.

## Public interfaces and contracts
- `VehicleTuning` (serializable, `Validate()`): mass/CoM, suspension (rest length, wheel radius, spring, damping), engine/brake/reverse/rolling resistance, steering lock (low/high speed), front/rear/handbrake grip, grip cap, boost (force, max speed, energy, regen, cooldown), air assist, downforce, flip reset time.
- `VehicleMath` (pure): `SuspensionForce` (never pulls), `SteerAngleDegrees`, `DriveForce`, `LateralGripForce` (capped so tyres slide).
- `BoostSystem` (deterministic energy/cooldown), `FlipRecovery` (reset after sustained flip at low speed).
- `ArcadeVehicle` (MonoBehaviour): `SetInput(DrivingInput)`, `ResetTo(position, rotation)`, read-only `GroundedWheels`, `ForwardSpeed`, `IsBoosting`, `BoostEnergy`, `Resets`. Non-allocating raycasts ignore the car's own colliders.
- Assets: `Prefabs/ArcadeCar.prefab`, `Scenes/TestTrack.unity` (dev/test scene, not in build settings).

## Failure modes / safety
- Invalid tuning or missing wheel anchors disable the component with an error instead of simulating garbage.
- NaN/negative inputs are neutralised in the math; suspension never produces pulling forces; grip forces are capped.
- Flip recovery resets upright after 2 s stuck on its side/roof at low speed.

## Tests
- EditMode (`VehicleMathTests`, `BoostSystemTests`): suspension sign/monotonicity, steering narrowing, top-speed fade, brake → reverse, throttle-while-rolling-back brakes, rolling resistance, grip cap and drift, NaN safety, boost drain/cooldown/regen/determinism, flip recovery timing, tuning validation.
- PlayMode (`ArcadeVehicleStabilityTests`): **DoD** at **30 Hz and 60 Hz** physics steps on `TestTrack`:
  - settle on 4 wheels, level, vertical speed < 0.05 m/s;
  - full throttle over 6 speed bumps and a 14° jump (must go airborne, then pass z > 120); speed ≤ top speed + 1.5 m/s;
  - boost exceeds top speed but stays ≤ boost cap + 1.5 m/s (measured: exactly 36.0 m/s);
  - brakes stop the car within 5 s, then it rolls to rest;
  - throughout: no NaN, never below the track, angular speed < 10 rad/s, upright > 0.8 on four wheels, zero flip resets.
  - Handbrake test: same steering input yields > 1.15× the yaw rate with the handbrake, without spin-out or flips.

## Bugs found during validation (fixed)
1. `TestScenes.UnloadAll` unloaded the test runner's `InitTestScene` when a vehicle test ran first, stalling the whole PlayMode run silently. It now keeps that scene.
2. With no pedal held the car coasted forever; added `RollingResistance`.
3. The original test held the brake for a fixed time, which (by design) turns into reverse at a standstill; the test now brakes until stopped, then releases.
4. The track ground ended 6 m past the stopping point; extended to avoid a flaky fall-off.
