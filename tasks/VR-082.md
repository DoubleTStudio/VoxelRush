## Bugs found and fixed
1. **Edit intents from clients could make the server's decoder throw** (`ArgumentException` from `new Guid()` on short input) instead of rejecting: exact length check (41 bytes). Results with unknown rejection codes are refused (they'd later throw in `Describe`/`ReasonCode`); a test ties the limit to the enum.
2. **World-info and chunk-snapshot decoders threw `EndOfStreamException`/`ArgumentException`** on truncated input, which the client's handlers don't catch: now only `InvalidDataException`.
3. (Fixed in VR-062, found by its CI) honest clients kicked for "input abuse" when the server lagged.

## Tests and evidence
- **Destruction stress** `tools/stress-destruction.sh native`, 4 runs: 107–119 acknowledged edits, all present after the SIGKILL (per chunk, recovered revision ≥ highest acknowledged; 112–123 records recovered, incl. collapse batches and durable-but-unacknowledged edits), towers collapsed under load, late joiner equals the recovered server, worst window p95 1.2–2.2 ms, 0 overruns, no alerts on the restarted server.
- **Fuzzing** (`DecoderFuzzTests`, 4,000 seeded inputs per decoder: random, bit flips, byte swaps, truncations, extensions, hostile 0xFFFF lengths) over connection payloads, join tickets (no mutated ticket ever verifies), race state/track/progress/results, journal records/deltas (accepted records stay in bounds), world info, chunk snapshots; `EditWireFuzzTests` (20,000 inputs).
- **Storage faults** (`StorageFaultTests`): disk full and fsync failure stop acknowledgements (failed batch never reported durable), refuse further client edits and server changes (`persistence_backpressure`); torn write truncated on recovery, only flushed records survive.
- **Secret scan**: gitleaks over the full history — clean (one dummy test credential allowlisted by exact value).
