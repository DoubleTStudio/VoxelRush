# Task: VR-023 — Streaming, collider revision and budget

## Goal
Load collidable terrain around cars within bounded per-tick work. DoD (BACKLOG): driving never enters unready chunk.

## Prerequisites / relevant docs
VR-020..VR-022, VR-011. VOXEL_ENGINE (Chunk lifecycle, streaming priorities, Collision: server collision-relevant chunks ready before entry, collider revision, no stale colliders), SAVE_GAME_CLOUD_PERSISTENCE (spawn only after collider-critical chunks are ready), AGENTS (bounded tick work).

## In scope / out of scope
In: engine-free planner + streamer (priorities, budgets, hysteresis, neighbour-complete colliders, collider revision + stale rebuild), `TravelGuard`, Unity `VoxelWorldStreamer` (MeshCollider per chunk, spawn gating, guard before physics), tests at domain and physics level.
Out: wiring the streamer into Server/Client/Bot scenes and the network tick (follow-up: replaces the flat Arena; needs ramps/slopes so arcade cars handle 1-block terraces), client render streaming (VoxelTerrainView streaming), async/jobs generation (VR-070), snapshot-backed sources (M3), memory caps by device tier (VR-071).

## Public interfaces and contracts
- World: `IChunkSource`, `IChunkBackend`, `StreamingObserver`, `StreamingSettings` (radius, hysteresis, lookahead, Y range, generate/collider/unload budgets), `ChunkStreamer` (`Step`, `StateOf`, `IsColumnReady`, `IsBlockColumnReady`, `ColliderRevision`, `RefreshStaleColliders`), `ChunkState` (Unloaded/Generated/Ready), `TravelGuard.Clamp`, `GeneratedChunkSource`.
- Gameplay: `VoxelWorldStreamer` (`Configure`, `Register/Unregister(ArcadeVehicle)`, `Step = StreamStep + GuardStep`, `IsReadyAt`, `GuardClamps`, `ColliderObjects`). Execution order −100 (before vehicles).
- Rules: a column is Ready only when every Y layer has a collider; colliders wait for desired face neighbours (no border walls); `GuardStep` must run every physics step regardless of streaming frequency.

## Bugs found during validation (fixed)
- **Guard coupled to streaming frequency**: with streaming throttled to every 15th physics step the guard also ran every 15th step and a car crossed into an unready column (`step 458: car at (29.5,128.2)`). Split into `StreamStep` (budgeted) and `GuardStep` (every step).
- Test design: "starved" budgets that weren't starved; a wide steering weave that circled inside loaded chunks; hysteresis test moved too far. Corrected with stated arithmetic.

## Tests
- Domain (`ChunkStreamingTests`): per-tick budgets; whole radius becomes ready, under-vehicle column first (negative coordinates); travel direction before behind; colliders wait for neighbours; unload hysteresis; collider revision + stale rebuild; guard axis/diagonal clamping; **engine-free DoD** at 28 m/s for 30 s: generous budget never holds, starved (streaming every 4th tick) holds; never enters an unready column.
- PlayMode (`VoxelStreamingDriveTests`): **DoD with real PhysX**: ArcadeCar on streamed MeshCollider voxel ground, 12 simulated seconds full throttle; spawn held until its column is ready; generous budget: no clamps, > 150 m driven; starved (streaming every 15th step): held by the guard; both: never in an unready column, never below ground, no flip resets.
