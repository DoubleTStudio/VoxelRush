# Task: DES-001 — Material catalog + HP data + sparse damage

## Goal
Block materials and per-block hit points. DoD (BACKLOG): material tests.

## Prerequisites / relevant docs
VR-020 (chunk data). GAMEPLAY_RULES (Damage: material HP, integer quantization), DESTRUCTION (HP: sparse per-chunk damage map, destroy at HP ≤ 0 → air), CONTROLS_ASSETS (12 block types), skills/destruction.

## In scope / out of scope
In: `BlockIds` for 12 block types, `BlockMaterial`/`MaterialCatalog` (validated, allocation-free lookup), `ChunkDamage` (sparse HP, integer damage, destroy → air, replace resets HP, restore with validation).
Out: damage sources (collision DES-003, explosions DES-004, mining), replicating/journaling HP (needs journal record v2 + v1 migration; with DES-003/004), zone rules (DES-002), debris (DES-005), atlas tiles/art for the new block types, mesher opacity from the catalog (glass).

## Public interfaces and contracts
- Fixed by spec: dirt 30, grass 35, wood 50, asphalt 100, stone 120, reinforced 300, protected indestructible (`ushort.MaxValue`, not buildable).
- **Placeholders (tune in playtests):** brick 90, concrete 150, glass 20 (non-opaque), sand 25, metal 200.
- `ChunkDamage.Apply(chunk, local, damage)`: damage ≤ 0, air, unknown and indestructible are ignored (no healing path); HP ≤ 0 destroys (block → air, chunk revision advances); damage-only changes don't advance the revision (HP replication is separate).

## Tests (`Tests/Domain/Destruction/MaterialTests.cs`)
Spec HP values; protected immune and not buildable; 12 named types with positive HP, air/unknown absent, glass non-opaque; catalog rejects duplicates/air/zero HP; damage stored sparsely; destroy at ≤ 0 incl. exactly lethal; protected/air/zero/negative ignored; `int.MaxValue` damage doesn't overflow; replacement resets HP; restore drops invalid entries; deterministic hit sequence.
