# Task: DES-012 — Mesh/collider rebuild priority under destruction

## Goal
When blocks change, colliders next to cars are rebuilt before the next physics step. DoD (BACKLOG): no stale collision.

## Prerequisites / relevant docs
VR-023 (streamer, collider revision, guard), DES-004/011 (bulk removals). VOXEL_ENGINE (Collision: collider revision, no stale colliders), DESTRUCTION (Tests: collision across chunk border), AGENTS (bounded tick work).

## In scope / out of scope
In: `ChunkStreamer.MarkChanged` (chunk + the neighbour across a touched border), `IsColliderCurrent`, `RefreshCritical` (car-adjacent stale chunks, incl. 0.5 s lookahead, nearest first, own budget of 8, deferred count), nearest-first background `RefreshStaleColliders(observers)`, `StreamingSettings` critical knobs; `VoxelWorldStreamer.GuardStep` runs the critical refresh every physics step, `SetBlock` marks changes.
Out: render-mesh priority on clients (the client doesn't render the synced plot yet; the same nearest-first rule applies when it does), wiring the replica/destruction into the streamer in networked scenes (with the arena replacement, VR-023 follow-up), background cooking on jobs (VR-070).

## Design notes
- Stale = collider revision ≠ chunk revision, or explicitly marked (border neighbours, whose own revision doesn't change). Chunk-level fallback still catches edits made without `MarkChanged`.
- Critical work is tied to the guard (every physics step), not to the throttled streaming step — the same lesson as VR-023's guard bug: under load streaming runs every Nth step, which is too late for a wall blown open in front of a car.
- Only chunks within 8 m of a car (or of where it will be in 0.5 s) are inspected, so the critical pass costs nothing when nothing nearby changed. Far changes use the normal collider budget, nearest first.

## Tests
- Domain (`ColliderRebuildPriorityTests`): border change marks the neighbour, interior doesn't; critical rebuilds only near the car, nearest first; lookahead along velocity; revision changes caught without marking; critical budget defers and counts; background nearest first within budget; unload forgets staleness; **DoD**: 28 m/s for 20 s with blocks removed every tick ahead of, beside and far behind the car and streaming every 4th tick → **zero** stale chunks within 8 m before any physics step, nothing deferred; control without the critical pass → stale chunks next to the car (the check detects the bug).
- PlayMode (`DestructionColliderTests`, real PhysX): **DoD** — car at full throttle toward a 3-high brick wall across a chunk border; the wall is removed ~5 m before impact right after a streaming step (streaming every 15th step) → the car drives through (> 10 m past, speed stays > 15 m/s). Control with the critical budget at 0 → the car hits the removed wall (speed < 10 m/s).
