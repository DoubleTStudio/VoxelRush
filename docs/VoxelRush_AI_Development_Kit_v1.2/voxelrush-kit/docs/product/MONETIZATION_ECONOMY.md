# Economy and Monetization (v1.2)
Core racing, building, destruction and standard vehicles remain free. Optional monetization: cosmetic skins/decorations, opt-in rewarded ads, later cosmetic-only season pass. No paid speed, damage, exclusive functional blocks or checkpoint advantages. Inventory, currency and entitlements are backend-authoritative; Apple/Google purchases must be verified server-side, with refunds/revocations and idempotent reward grants. Rewarded ad grants require verified completion where supported. Keep child-directed compliance, consent, regional policies, store requirements and age rating as launch gates. Track DAU, retention, ARPDAU, ad eCPM, conversion, server cost per DAU and world-storage costs. Do not add ads to active races.
